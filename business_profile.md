# On Us business profile

This is the working local profile for agent planning. Replace or expand the
open questions as the business becomes more defined.

- Business: On Us, a Black-centered, community-first social platform focused on
  connection, creativity, cultural expression, and empowerment.
- Offer: A local platform/community where Black people can connect, share
  stories and work, discover one another, and participate in community-led
  experiences. Confirm the current product features and primary conversion
  event before launching paid campaigns.
- Audience: Black Oakland and wider Bay Area residents, especially creatives,
  entrepreneurs, community leaders, and people looking for culturally relevant
  connection. Secondary audiences include existing event participants,
  supporters, and friends/family who can introduce new users.
- Campaign geography: Bay Area, with Oakland as the initial center of gravity.
- Brand voice: Community-first, warm, confident, specific, culturally
  respectful, and action-oriented. Lead with real people and concrete
  experiences instead of generic startup language.
- Current business objective: Grow qualified Bay Area users and meaningful
  participation on On Us. The conversion event, baseline counts, budget,
  launch date, and available creative assets still need confirmation.
- Past wins: Not provided yet. Do not invent testimonials, partnerships,
  user counts, event attendance, or performance claims.
- Things to never say: Do not claim On Us has launched, published, sent,
  booked, paid for, partnered with, or achieved anything unless the user
  provides evidence. Do not stereotype the audience, promise safety or
  outcomes without support, or use vague claims such as “revolutionary” or
  “game-changing” without proof.
